Questions

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We help clarify the requirements and the next steps.

Is hosting included?

Yes. Hosting and maintenance are included in the starter, bot and combo packages, and websites include your domain. No hidden fees.

Is there a minimum term?

Yes, the minimum term is 2 months. Details on cancellation are part of your written agreement.

Who owns my domain?

You do. Your domain is registered in your name, and we handle setup and renewals while your plan is active. The registration fees are covered by your monthly price. If you cancel, the domain stays yours.

Can I request additional features later?

Yes. Additional functionality can be scoped and quoted separately.

Can I connect my website to Discord?

Yes. The Ultimate Combo is built around that idea. Advanced integrations may require a separate quote.

What does a team portal cost?

It depends on the scope. Team portals and web apps get their own quote, which we discuss in the Discovery Call.
