Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. We only store in your browser's local storage that you have dismissed the privacy notice. This is technically necessary and contains no personal data.

Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The website is delivered via GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit, the host processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time and the page requested (server log files). The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and stable operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA rely on standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Domain (IONOS)

The domain is managed by IONOS SE (Germany) and forwards requests to our hosting.

Contact and inquiry forms (Formspree)

When you submit a form, we transmit your details (name, email, message and, optionally, how you heard about us) via Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA) to us by email. We use them to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed and no statutory retention duties apply.

Contact by email or phone

If you contact us by email or phone, we process your details to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). Our mailbox is hosted by Proton AG (Switzerland).

Booking (Calendly)

Our buttons lead to Calendly (Calendly LLC, USA). Your details (name, email, preferred time) are only processed there once you click the link and book an appointment. Calendly's privacy information applies.

Payments (Stripe)

For subscriptions we link to Stripe payment pages (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd., Ireland). You enter payment data only there; we do not receive it. Stripe's privacy information applies.

External links and social networks

Links to Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn and other sites are simple links. Data is only transferred once you click a link; the respective provider's privacy policy then applies.
